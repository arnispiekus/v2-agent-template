# Hand over from files alone

Start a fresh session without chat history, on the other harness when available.
Read STATE, the issue and the files it names. Answer with file references and
evidence status. Say unknown when the files cannot answer.

1. What is the goal, and who is it for?
2. What state is the work in right now?
3. What is the next step, and who does it?
4. What does the owner need to do that only they can do?
5. What was the last decision, and why?
6. How will we prove it works for the person it is for?

Repair missing facts in the appropriate files before claiming this passed.
Record session, date and answers without private data. A builder rereading their
own files does not establish the fresh-session proof.
