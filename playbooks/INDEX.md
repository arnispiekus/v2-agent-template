# Playbooks

- [New project](new-project.md): size work and agree a measure of done.
- [Loop](loop.md): build, check, approve, prove and update files.
- [Find the cause](find-the-cause.md): investigate before fixing a failure.
- [Test first](test-first.md): show meaningful failure before changing behaviour.
- [Hand-over](hand-over.md): answer six questions from files alone.

Use the smallest process that gives adequate evidence and owner control.
Adapted methods are attributed in [NOTICE](../NOTICE.md).
