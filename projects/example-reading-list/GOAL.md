# A reading list the reader can trust

- Person: a reader who loses track of books they intend to read.
- Need: add a title, see unread titles and mark a title as read.
- Outcome: choose the next book without rebuilding a list.
- Their measure of done: "I can add three titles, mark one read, reopen the app
  and find exactly two unread titles and one read title without help."
- Reference inputs: invented titles First Lantern, Quiet Orchard and Paper Kite.
- Reference figures: three total; two unread and one read after marking one.
- Out of scope: accounts, sharing, recommendations and importing catalogues.
- Acceptance owner: the reader; the builder records observation in STATE.

This is fictional. No app or acceptance result is supplied.
